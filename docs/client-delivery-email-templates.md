## Sending checklist

- Choose the matching Full or Demo template.
- Replace every bracketed placeholder, including the report URL and exact expiration date, time, and time zone.
- Hyperlink the report text to the complete protected URL, including everything after `#`.
- Keep the access code out of the delivery email; send it separately.
- Preview all links and remove optional attachment language unless the files are actually attached.
- For a Full report presentation after the expiration deadline, manually extend access in Atlas. The email does not trigger an automatic extension.
- These are reusable one-to-one delivery drafts, not scheduled emails or marketing workflows. HubSpot template creation is pending access to the sales-template editor.
